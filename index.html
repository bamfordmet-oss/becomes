What is a prebiotic, and how is it different from a probiotic?

A prebiotic is a fibre your body doesn't digest but your gut bacteria do. Probiotics are the live bacteria; prebiotics are what they feed on. Ours is FOS, a soluble fibre found naturally in plants.

How much fibre do I need a day?

Food labels use a daily value of 28 g, and most people eat less than that. One spoon of Prebiotic Syrup gives you 14.3 g, about half, alongside the fruit, vegetables and whole grains you already eat.

Is it a healthy sugar alternative for tea and coffee?

Yes. It's lightly sweet and stirs into tea, coffee, porridge or yoghurt. One spoon has 1.6 g of sugars and 35 calories. A tablespoon of honey has about 17 g of sugars.
