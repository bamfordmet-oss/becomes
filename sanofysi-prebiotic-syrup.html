Questions

Good to know.

Want the longer answer? Read what a prebiotic is and how much fibre you need a day.

What is prebiotic syrup, and what is it used for?

Sanofysi Prebiotic Syrup is a liquid fibre supplement made with 95% FOS, a soluble prebiotic fibre. It is used to add fibre to an everyday diet: you stir one 15 ml spoon into tea, coffee, porridge, yoghurt or a smoothie, and it provides 14.3 g of fibre.

What is FOS (fructooligosaccharides), and is it good or bad for you?

FOS, also called oligofructose, is a soluble fibre found naturally in onions, garlic, bananas and chicory. Your body does not digest it; your gut bacteria do. It is widely used in foods. As with any fibre, taking a lot at once can cause gas or bloating, so build up gradually.

Is a prebiotic the same as a probiotic?

No. Probiotics are live bacteria, found in yoghurt, fermented foods and some supplements. Prebiotics are the fibres those bacteria feed on. This syrup is a prebiotic.

What are the benefits of prebiotic fibre?

Prebiotic fibre feeds the beneficial bacteria in your gut and counts towards your daily fibre intake. Most adults eat less fibre than the recommended 25 to 30 g a day, and one spoon of this syrup provides 14.3 g.

Is prebiotic fibre soluble or insoluble?

FOS is a soluble fibre. It dissolves completely in liquid, which is why the syrup stirs into hot and cold drinks without grit.

How much fibre do I need a day, and how much is in one spoon?

The World Health Organization recommends at least 25 g of fibre a day for adults, and food labels use a daily value of 28 g. One 15 ml spoon of Prebiotic Syrup has 14.3 g, which is 52% of the daily value.

How do I take it, and when is the best time to take a prebiotic?

Stir up to one 15 ml spoon a day into a drink or breakfast. There is no single best time: most people take it in the morning with tea, coffee or porridge so it becomes a habit. If you are new to fibre, start with a teaspoon and build up over one to two weeks.

Can I take prebiotics and probiotics together?

Yes. They do different jobs and are often taken together: probiotics are live bacteria, and prebiotics are the fibre they feed on.

Can prebiotics cause gas, bloating or diarrhoea?

They can if you take a lot of fibre at once, especially if you are not used to it. Start with a teaspoon, increase slowly over one to two weeks and drink plenty of water. If symptoms continue, stop and speak to a health professional.

Does fibre help with digestion and constipation?

Fibre is a normal part of healthy digestion and health bodies recommend eating enough of it alongside plenty of fluids. Prebiotic Syrup is a food supplement that adds fibre to your diet. It is not a medicine or a treatment for constipation; if constipation persists, see a doctor.

Is prebiotic syrup a healthy sugar alternative for tea and coffee?

It is a lower-sugar way to sweeten a drink. It tastes mildly sweet, less sweet than sugar, and one 15 ml spoon has 1.6 g of sugars and 35 calories. A tablespoon of white sugar has about 12 g of sugars and a tablespoon of honey about 17 g.

Is it better than honey or sugar in tea?

Spoon for spoon it has far less sugar than either and adds 14.3 g of fibre, which sugar and honey do not contain. It is also less sweet, so some people use half their usual sugar plus a spoon of syrup.

Does it contain sugar, and how many calories does it have?

One 15 ml spoon has 1.6 g of total sugars and 35 calories. Most of the 16 g of carbohydrate is fibre.

What does it taste like, and can I use it in hot drinks, oatmeal and smoothies?

It tastes lightly sweet with no aftertaste, and it dissolves in hot and cold liquids. Use it in tea, coffee, oatmeal or other porridge, yoghurt and smoothies.

Is this liquid fibre supplement for adults, women and men? Can children take it?

It is made for adults of any gender. Ask a health professional before giving a fibre supplement to a child.

Is it plant-based?

Yes. Prebiotic Syrup is 100% plant-based.

How long does a bottle last, and how should I store it?

A 290 ml bottle holds about 19 servings, so around 19 days at one spoon a day. Keep it closed in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and use it within the date on the label.

Anyone who is pregnant or breastfeeding, taking medication, managing blood sugar or diabetes, or living with a digestive condition such as IBS.
